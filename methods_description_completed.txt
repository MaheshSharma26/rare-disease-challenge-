[Team Name]
Antigravity Genomics

[Model/Approach Description]
Integrated multi-omics variant prioritization pipeline. Scanned whole-genome VCF (GRCh38) combining positional exon/CDS mapping, ClinVar pathogenic cross-referencing, Ensembl VEP molecular consequence prediction, dbSNP/gnomAD population allele frequency filtering, and read-backed haplotype phasing (PGT/PID) to identify compound heterozygous disease-causing alleles in rare disease genes.

[Automated Output vs Manual Curation]
Automated computational variant extraction and prioritization pipeline followed by targeted clinical genetics curation and genotype-phenotype correlation against HPO term profiles.

[Manual Review Description]
Validated candidate variants by verifying sequencing depth (DP), genotype quality (GQ), allele balance (AD), read-backed phasing, ACMG pathogenicity criteria, and disease-gene fit against Mosaic Variegated Aneuploidy (MVA) syndrome 1 (BUB1B) and hereditary cancer/thrombophilia risk factors.

[Public Data Used]
Publicly available databases: NCBI ClinVar, NCBI dbSNP, Ensembl VEP (GRCh38), gnomAD v4, MyVariant.info, HPO (Human Phenotype Ontology), OMIM, and GENCODE v46.

[Proprietary Data Used]
None. 100% publicly available data and tools.

[Compound Heterozygous Support]
Yes. The pipeline explicitly identifies and pairs candidate compound heterozygous variants on the same locus (e.g. BUB1B) and evaluates read-backed phasing (PGT/PID) and distance metrics to confirm trans configuration.

[Secondary / Incidental Findings]
Filtered for incidental/co-diagnosed pathogenic variants. Identified a secondary heterozygous Factor V Leiden variant in F5 (chr1:169549811 C>T, rs6025, p.Arg506Gln), providing valuable insight for clinical thromboembolism risk management during oncological care.

[Run Time & Cost]
Total runtime < 5 minutes on standard workstation. Total cost: $0.

[Abstract]
We present an automated, phenotype-driven genomic variant prioritization framework for rare disease diagnosis. Applied to Whole Genome Sequencing (WGS) of a proband presenting with rhabdomyosarcoma, nephrocalcinosis, short stature, failure to thrive, 32-week prematurity, and parental recurrent miscarriages, our pipeline identified a definitive compound heterozygous etiology in BUB1B: a pathogenic nonsense mutation (chr15:40209701 T>G, rs759242053, c.2210T>G, p.Leu737*) and a novel deleterious missense mutation (chr15:40220612 T>G, c.3006T>G, p.Asn1002Lys), diagnostic of Mosaic Variegated Aneuploidy (MVA) Syndrome 1 (OMIM #257300). In addition, an incidental finding in F5 (Factor V Leiden, rs6025) was surfaced as a secondary thrombophilia risk factor. Key pipeline strengths include rapid whole-genome CDS mapping, rigorous multi-database annotation, and automated recessive disease model recovery.
