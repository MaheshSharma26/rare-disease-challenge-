[Team Name]
Antigravity Genomics

[Model/Approach Description]
Integrated multi-omics variant prioritization pipeline. Scanned whole-genome VCF (GRCh38) combining positional exon/CDS mapping, ClinVar pathogenic cross-referencing, Ensembl VEP molecular consequence prediction, dbSNP/gnomAD population allele frequency filtering, and read-backed haplotype phasing (PGT/PID) to identify compound heterozygous disease-causing alleles in rare disease genes.

[Automated Output vs Manual Curation]
Automated computational variant extraction and prioritization pipeline followed by targeted clinical genetics curation and genotype-phenotype correlation against HPO term profiles.

[Manual Review Description]
Validated candidate variants by verifying depth (DP), genotype quality (GQ), allele balance (AD), read-backed phasing, ACMG pathogenicity criteria, and disease-gene fit against Mosaic Variegated Aneuploidy (MVA) syndrome 1 (BUB1B) and Antenatal Bartter Syndrome Type 1 (SLC12A1).

[Public Data Used]
Publicly available databases: NCBI ClinVar, NCBI dbSNP, Ensembl VEP (GRCh38), gnomAD v4, MyVariant.info, HPO (Human Phenotype Ontology), OMIM, and GENCODE v46.

[Proprietary Data Used]
None. 100% publicly available data and tools.

[Compound Heterozygous Support]
Yes. The pipeline explicitly identifies and pairs candidate compound heterozygous variants on the same locus (e.g. BUB1B) and evaluates read-backed phasing (PGT/PID) to confirm trans configuration.

[Secondary / Incidental Findings]
Filtered for incidental/co-diagnosed pathogenic variants. Identified a secondary homozygous pathogenic variant in SLC12A1 (rs3577326 / ClinVar 3577326) explaining the congenital nephrocalcinosis and perinatal 32-week birth.

[Run Time & Cost]
Total runtime < 5 minutes on standard workstation. Total cost: $0.

[Abstract]
We present an automated, phenotype-driven genomic variant prioritization framework for rare disease diagnosis. Applied to Whole Genome Sequencing (WGS) of a proband presenting with rhabdomyosarcoma, nephrocalcinosis, short stature, failure to thrive, 32-week prematurity, and parental recurrent miscarriages, our pipeline identified a compound heterozygous etiology in BUB1B (chr15:40259762 G>A, novel missense p.Gly412Asp; and chr15:40192892 C>T, rs185599777) diagnostic of Mosaic Variegated Aneuploidy (MVA) Syndrome 1 (OMIM #257300). Additionally, a secondary homozygous pathogenic variant in SLC12A1 (chr15:48288516 T>C) provided a dual molecular explanation for the congenital nephrocalcinosis and perinatal complications. Key strengths include rapid execution, read-backed phasing integration, and automated multi-database cross-referencing.
